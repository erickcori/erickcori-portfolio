👋 Welcome

I’m a Peruvian chemical engineer based in Brazil, with extensive experience in the water and sanitation sector across Latin America. I hold an MSc in Water, Sanitation and Health Engineering from the University of Leeds (UK), and a second MSc in Sustainability from the University of São Paulo (USP).

🧠 Academic & Professional Background

I’ve led over 20 public water and sanitation investment projects in Peru, served as General Manager of EMSAPA CALCA, taught Wastewater Treatment at the National University of Callao, and consulted for Hydroconseil (France) supporting the IDB and OLAS initiative.

🧪 Current Research

I am currently pursuing a PhD focused on:
“Multi-objective Optimization of Wastewater Treatment Plants Using Reinforcement Learning: A Simulation-Based and Sustainability-Oriented Approach.”
This project explores the use of RL algorithms within the BSM1 model to optimize energy, effluent quality, and emissions.
